# Fact check — every claim in the video

Each number spoken in the narration, with its source and how much weight it bears.
**Confidence** is my assessment of how defensible the figure is if a viewer
challenges it in the comments.

| ✅ | solid — primary source or broad agreement |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ | **contested or range-dependent — the script hedges deliberately** |

## Framing claims (interpretation, not fact)

These are the argumentative spine of the video. They are **opinion supported by
the facts above**, and should be defended as analysis rather than data:

1. Diversifying fabs does not diversify the ASML chokepoint — it adds customers to it.
2. Export controls create an incentive to route around them ("impossible turned out to mean expensive").
3. The fragility is a *product* of successful optimisation, not a failure of it.

## Things a sharp commenter may raise

- **"TSMC's share is 38%, not 70%."** Different market definition — see Act III note. Both are defensible; the video states which it uses.
- **"High-NA costs $380M, not €350M."** Currency and configuration differ; the script says "over €350 million" and the card notes prices vary.
- **"Japan/China also make lithography tools."** True for DUV and older nodes (Canon, Nikon, SMEE). The claim is specifically about **EUV**, where ASML is sole supplier. The script is careful to say "the most advanced."
- **"3nm isn't 3nm."** The video says this itself.
